\section{Current Implementation Boundaries}
\label{sec:implementation_gap_audit}
% ============================================================

This chapter records active claim boundaries rather than a history of closed
bugs.  Source code, result validity fields, and registered tests remain
authoritative.

\begin{longtable}{L{0.24\linewidth}L{0.62\linewidth}}
\toprule
Area & Current boundary \\
\midrule
\endhead
Projection & Public PF/OPF/TSPF GUI results use authored-space attribution. Projection certificates and provenance maps must survive expansion, bus merge, and dead-island deletion. Audit-only attribution is not a solved observable. \\
Parameters & The standard parameter library is externally editable and explicitly applied. Importers do not silently fill missing engineering data. Solver-specific request options require module-level effective-value echo. \\
Time series/annual & Per-step rich attribution and system snapshots are retained when requested. Dropped/non-converged steps must be disclosed; static-system substitution is not an acceptable dynamic fallback. \\
Carbon & Static and dynamic ledgers must expose conservation and storage recursion residuals. A residual correction must be labeled and cannot replace the physical energy recursion. \\
Transient & The phasor-domain solver records voltage, frequency, device telemetry, and events. Dynamic branch arrows are unsupported unless terminal current or P/Q is recorded by the dynamic solver. External GridLAB-D/OpenDSS comparisons certify only their shared model scope. \\
Short circuit & AC/DC and sequence/phase approximations are documented in the short-circuit reference; missing rich-device sequence data may limit fidelity. \\
Harmonics & Multiple harmonic formulations exist with declared resource and phase scope. Theory documents include target models that are not all production-enabled. \\
Reliability & Results expose method/model scope and solver validity. Metrics from different physical scopes must not be compared as though they share one exact formulation. \\
GUI & \texttt{Canvas.renderFrame} updates result overlays only. Static PF result display and dynamic playback are separate paths; playback cannot mutate authored component parameters. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Known Numerical Issue}

The default transient initialization of \texttt{power\_system.json} can expose
an extreme DC voltage under the voltage-health guard. This is a dynamic
model/initialization issue. It must not be hidden by the Canvas renderer or by
inventing static branch flows.

\subsection{Maintenance Rule}

Resolved defect narratives belong in version control and regression tests, not
in this chapter. Add an item here only when it changes the interpretation or
validity of a current result.
