\section{Engine Solver System}
\label{sec:engine-solver-system}

The \texttt{hacdcpf::engine} namespace in this repository is mainly a
compatibility facade over \texttt{mipsolvers::engine}.  The local headers keep
historical include paths stable while importing engine symbols with \texttt{using}
declarations.  Current implementation files for LCQP, dual simplex, branch and
cut, and external adapters live in the \texttt{mipsolvers} dependency, not under
\texttt{src/engine} in this repository.

\subsection{Forwarded Engine Surface}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.30\linewidth}L{0.56\linewidth}}
\toprule
Local forwarding header & Imported symbols / role \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{engine/problem\_types.hpp} & Problem-class enums, symbolic model structs, LP/QP/MIP/NLP/MINLP model types, \texttt{SolveStats}, and \texttt{SolveResult}. \\
\texttt{engine/kernel/ipm/lcqp\_solver.hpp} & \texttt{LCQPOptions} and \texttt{NativeLCQPAdapter}. \\
\texttt{engine/kernel/lp\_kernel/dual\_simplex.hpp} & Simplex options, basis/result types, \texttt{StandardFormLP}, and \texttt{solve\_lp\_with\_basis}. \\
\texttt{engine/kernel/linear\_algebra/linear\_solver.hpp} & Sparse solver abstraction and Eigen-based sparse solver factories. \\
\texttt{engine/kernel/globalization/globalization.hpp} & Dogleg, trust-region update, and pseudo-transient continuation helpers. \\
\texttt{engine/solver/external/adapters.hpp} & \texttt{HighsAdapter}, \texttt{IpoptAdapter}, \texttt{ScipAdapter}, and \texttt{GurobiAdapter}. \\
\texttt{engine/branch\_and\_cut.hpp} & Branching and node-selection enums plus B\&C statistics/result/option types. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

There are no local PDLP or LP-IPM backends documented by the current headers.
The backends visible through this compatibility layer are LCQP, dual simplex,
HiGHS, Ipopt, SCIP, and Gurobi, subject to the configuration of
\texttt{mipsolvers} and the optional external packages available at build time.

\subsection{Local Newton Solver Class}

The one local engine-style solver declaration is
\texttt{include/hacdcpf/engine/solver/native/nle/newton\_solver.hpp}.  Its
implementation is \texttt{src/power\_flow/newton\_solver.cpp}; it is the hybrid
PF Newton class used by the power-flow module.  The file is not an engine
implementation directory and does not define the optimization-engine LCQP or LP
backends.
