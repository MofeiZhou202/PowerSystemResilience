\section{Information Models and Field Dictionaries}
\label{sec:components}

\subsection{Modeling Layers Used by the Code}

The code base uses three distinct model layers.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Public information model}: the rich structs under \texttt{include/hacdcpf/model/} used by I/O, case builders, and external callers.
  \item \textbf{Canonical steady-state model}: the projected subset generated by \texttt{project\_to\_canonical\_models}, where rich components are converted into equivalent loads, branches, or aggregated injections.
  \item \textbf{Solver assembly model}: \texttt{powerflow::SolverData}, which stores contiguous vectors, aggregated per-bus injections, ZIP coefficients, and sparse matrices needed by the PF and OPF kernels.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{EnergyRouter Multi-Port Model}
\label{sec:er-multiport}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

An \texttt{EnergyRouter} is a power-electronic converter hub that simultaneously
connects to an arbitrary number of AC and DC buses.  Each physical terminal is
represented by one \texttt{EnergyRouterPort} entry in the router's
\texttt{ports} vector.

\subsubsection*{Port attributes}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.22\linewidth}L{0.20\linewidth}L{0.50\linewidth}}
\toprule
Field & Type / default & Description \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{port\_type} & \texttt{ERPortType::AC} & \texttt{AC} or \texttt{DC}: determines which bus network this port injects into \\
\texttt{side} & \texttt{int\{0\}} & Physical winding side: 0 = Side A, 1 = Side B (used by SST/UPFC topologies) \\
\texttt{bus} & \texttt{int\{0\}} & Index of the connected AC or DC bus \\
\texttt{p\_mw}, \texttt{q\_mvar} & \texttt{double\{0\}} & Bus-injection sign convention: positive = power into bus \\
\texttt{eta} & \texttt{double\{0.98\}} & Per-port conversion efficiency for loss allocation \\
\texttt{control\_mode} & \texttt{ERControlMode::PQ} & Per-port control law: \texttt{PQ} (fixed setpoint), \texttt{VQ} (voltage-droop reactive), or \texttt{Slack} (voltage-forming) \\
\texttt{p\_set\_mw}, \texttt{q\_set\_mvar} & \texttt{double\{0\}} & Active/reactive setpoints when \texttt{control\_mode = PQ} \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection*{Multi-port power balance}

Let port~$k$ carry active injection $P_k$ (positive = into bus) and efficiency
$\eta_k$.  The lossless internal balance constraint is:
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{k=1}^{N_p} P_k \;=\; 0 \qquad \text{(lossless ER)}.
  \label{eq:er-balance-lossless}
\end{equation}
When per-port efficiency $\eta_k < 1$, the actual balance allocates losses to
each port proportionally:
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{k : P_k < 0} |P_k| \;=\; \sum_{k : P_k > 0} \frac{P_k}{\eta_k}
  \qquad \text{(lossy ER, simplified allocation)}.
  \label{eq:er-balance-lossy}
\end{equation}
In the current projection path, an \texttt{EnergyRouter} is expanded into a
set of internal DC buses, VSC equivalents for each AC port, and DCDC converter
equivalents for each DC port.  The internal bus forms the power-exchange node;
\eqref{eq:er-balance-lossless} is enforced implicitly by the solver topology.

\subsection{VirtualPowerPlant Fleet Aggregation Model}
\label{sec:vpp-model}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

A \texttt{VirtualPowerPlant} (VPP) represents a portfolio of distributed
energy resources (DERs) co-dispatched as a single grid entity.  The struct
carries both static fleet composition data and dynamic dispatch outputs.

\subsubsection*{Fleet composition}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.30\linewidth}L{0.15\linewidth}L{0.47\linewidth}}
\toprule
Field & Default & Meaning \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{n\_pv\_systems} & 0 & Number of rooftop/ground-mounted PV units in portfolio \\
\texttt{n\_wind\_turbines} & 0 & Number of wind turbines \\
\texttt{n\_battery\_systems} & 0 & Number of battery storage units \\
\texttt{n\_ev\_chargers} & 0 & Number of V2G-capable EV chargers \\
\texttt{n\_controllable\_loads} & 0 & Demand-response resources \\
\texttt{n\_chp} & 0 & Combined heat-and-power units \\
\texttt{n\_biomass}, \texttt{n\_thermal\_storage}, \texttt{n\_hvac}, \texttt{n\_industrial} & 0 & Other flexible DER categories \\
\texttt{p\_pv\_sum\_mw}, \texttt{p\_wind\_sum\_mw} & 0 & Aggregated renewable capacity \\
\texttt{e\_storage\_sum\_mwh} & 0 & Total usable storage energy \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection*{Aggregation dispatch model}

Let $P_i^{\mathrm{DER}}$ be the net active output of DER unit~$i$ (positive =
generation, negative = consumption).  The VPP aggregates to:
\begin{equation}
  P^{\mathrm{VPP}} \;=\; \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} P_i^{\mathrm{DER}}
  \;=\; P^{\mathrm{gen}} - P^{\mathrm{flex,abs}},
  \label{eq:vpp-agg}
\end{equation}
where $P^{\mathrm{gen}}$ is the total scheduled generation and
$P^{\mathrm{flex,abs}}$ is the absorbed flexible load.
The field \texttt{p\_output\_mw} stores this aggregated value;
\texttt{q\_output\_mvar} carries the corresponding reactive output.

Regulation capability is bounded by:
\begin{equation}
  -P^{\mathrm{reg,down}} \;\le\; \Delta P \;\le\; P^{\mathrm{reg,up}},
  \label{eq:vpp-reg}
\end{equation}
stored in \texttt{p\_regulation\_down\_mw} and \texttt{p\_regulation\_up\_mw}.

\subsubsection*{Projection to grid model}

In the current solver path, a \texttt{VirtualPowerPlant} is projected to a
single AC \texttt{StaticGenerator} at \texttt{aggregation\_bus} with:
\[
  p_{\mathrm{sg}} = \texttt{p\_output\_mw}, \quad
  q_{\mathrm{sg}} = \texttt{q\_output\_mvar}.
\]
Ramp-rate fields (\texttt{ramp\_up\_max\_mw\_min},
\texttt{ramp\_down\_max\_mw\_min}) and regulation bounds are available to
time-series and OPF formulations but are not yet enforced by the snapshot PF
solver.
